How to play Fevga

The race

  • Each side has 15 checkers, all starting on one point. Yours start in the far right corner; the computer's start in the near left corner. Choose white (ivory, the default) or black under You play — the board turns so your home is always near right.
  • Both sides travel counter-clockwise. You move right → left along the far side, then left → right along the near side into your home board (near right), and bear off.
  • Roll two dice and move by each number. Doubles are played four times.
  • Who starts: each side throws one die and the higher starts — by rolling again, or (Settings → Play) by playing those two opening dice as the first move.

Fevga's special rules

  • No hitting. A single checker owns its point: nobody may land on a point the opponent occupies.
  • The runner. Until your first checker has passed the computer's starting point, you may move only that checker.
  • Six in a row is allowed anywhere except your starting quarter (below).
  • Unblocking. If every one of your opponent's checkers on the board is stuck on the single point right behind your six in a row, your move must open one of those six points so they get a chance to move. (Playing as many dice as you can still comes first.)
  • Keep your starting quarter open. You may never end a move holding all six points of your starting quarter (your points 1–6). One checker may pass over the last open point on its way (1→3→5 with two 2s is fine).
  • Play both dice when you can; if only one can be played, it must be the larger.

Bearing off & scoring

  • Once all 15 are in your home board, bear off by exact roll, or with a higher number from your highest point.
  • First to bear off all 15 wins 1 point; mars (the loser has borne off none) scores 2.
  • Match play (Settings → Play): games continue until one side reaches 5 points.

Opponents

  • 1 Beginner – random · 2 Novice – careless pip-counter · 3 Intermediate – blocks and races · 4 Advanced – looks one roll ahead · 5 Expert – deepest search.
